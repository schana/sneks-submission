# Sneks Privacy Policy & Terms of Service (Educational Use)

Last Updated: 2026-02-01

## 1. Our Commitment to Student Privacy

Sneks is designed with a "Privacy First" architecture. We acknowledge that our users include students under the age of
18\. We explicitly do not collect, store, or process Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from student users. We
are compliant with the principles of **COPPA** (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and **FERPA** (Family
Educational Rights and Privacy Act).

## 2. Information We Collect (The "No-PII" Standard)

To minimize risk and ensure compliance, Sneks operates on a strict **Anonymous/Generic Account** model for educational
events:

- **No Personal Accounts**: We do not allow student users to create accounts using personal email addresses, full names,
  or Google/Microsoft Oauth linking.
- **Generic Credentials**: Access is granted solely through generic, non-identifiable usernames (e.g., Contest_Team_01)
  created and distributed by the participating School Official or Teacher.
- **Data Collected**:
    - **Login Credentials**: Generic username and password (managed by the Teacher).
    - **User Submissions**: Source code submitted for the contest.
    - **Technical Logs**: Timestamps and temporary IP logs required for security and abuse prevention.

**We do not collect**: Student names, email addresses, physical addresses, phone numbers, or biometric data.

## 3. Ownership of Student Work (Intellectual Property)

We respect the intellectual property rights of student creators.

- **Student Ownership**: Students (or the School, depending on local policy) retain full copyright and ownership of the
  source code they submit to the platform.
- **Limited License**: By submitting code, the user grants Sneks a temporary, non-exclusive, royalty-free license
  strictly to compile, execute, and score the code within our sandboxed environment.
- **No Claim of Ownership**: Sneks makes no claim of ownership over student submissions. We will not publish, sell, or
  distribute student code to third parties.

## 4. Data Usage & Third Parties

- **No Advertising**: We do not display advertisements. We do not use data to build behavioral profiles.
- **No Data Sale**: We never sell, rent, or trade any user data to third parties.
- **Purpose Limitation**: Data is used strictly to rank contest submissions and display a scoreboard.

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

- **Contest Duration**: Data is retained only for the duration of the contest and a brief review period thereafter (
  typically 30 days) to allow for dispute resolution.
- **Automatic Purge**: Upon the conclusion of the retention period, all generic accounts and submitted code are
  permanently deleted from our servers.
- **Teacher Request**: A School Official may request the immediate deletion of contest data at any time by contacting
  admin@sneks.dev.

## 6. Security

We utilize industry-standard security measures to protect the integrity of the contest:

- **Encryption**: All traffic is encrypted in transit via HTTPS (TLS 1.2+) and at rest using AES-256.
- **Sandboxing**: Student code is executed in isolated, ephemeral containers that are destroyed immediately after
  execution.
